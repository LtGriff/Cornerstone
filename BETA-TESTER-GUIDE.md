# Cornerstone friend beta — quick guide

Open https://ltgriff.github.io/Cornerstone/ and choose **Start creating**. The example is an original-visual preview. Choose **Select my albums** to search by artist, or **Create by color** for a color wall. Album names come from MusicBrainz; the square designs in this beta are original stand-ins, not official album covers.

Pick four cornerstones in order: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. Add more favorites, then create your wall. In the editor you can try arrangements, replace an album, swap tiles in Manual mode, adjust the four corner colors, and choose the inner wallpaper frame. Download a phone or desktop wallpaper if you like. These downloads contain original visuals or colors only.

Your project is saved in **this browser on this device**. It does not sync to your other devices. Use **Save project file** to keep a backup and **Open project file** to restore it. A private/incognito window or cleared browser storage may lose the local save.

Spotify connection is paused in this shared build until an approved real-account test passes. There is no Apple Music account connection. Search may be slow or occasionally unavailable because the external artist catalog can be busy; wait and try again. Some albums may not appear.

Tap **Cornerstone BETA** for the current limitations. Tap **Send feedback** to create a copyable note, then send it to Tre through a channel you already use. The website does not transmit that note. Please do not include credentials, private listening history, or personal information.

When testing on a phone, also try scrolling the album picker, tapping two tiles to swap, reopening a saved project, and downloading a wallpaper. Tell Tre which browser/device you used if a step fails.
