# Cornerstone friend beta — quick guide

Open https://ltgriff.github.io/Cornerstone/ and choose **Start creating**. Choose **Select my albums** to search by artist, **Connect my music** if Tre has added your Spotify account as an approved tester, or **Create by color** for a color wall. Manual-search album names come from MusicBrainz; cover images come from Cover Art Archive when available. Spotify imports show matching Spotify covers on the wall. Older saved Spotify projects may need a fresh connection to retrieve cover URLs.

Pick four cornerstones in order: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. Add more favorites, then create your wall. In the editor you can try arrangements, replace an album, swap tiles in Manual mode, adjust the four corner colors, and choose the inner wallpaper frame. Wallpaper downloads include the chosen album covers when those images can be loaded for export. External artwork can fail to load or export; try another cover or upload one you have permission to use.

Your project is saved in **this browser on this device**. It does not sync to your other devices. Use **Save project file** to keep a backup and **Open project file** to restore it. A private/incognito window or cleared browser storage may lose the local save.

Spotify connection is available for approved testers. One account has reported a successful return with matching album names, but the complete sign-in and recommendation workflow has not been independently validated. The app owner must have Spotify Premium, and your account must be on the app’s approved-user list. If Spotify rejects the connection, choose albums manually and tell Tre the error category without sharing any codes or tokens. Spotify import reads your top tracks and artists once; it does not keep a token or continuously sync. There is no Apple Music account connection. Search may be slow or occasionally unavailable because the external artist catalog can be busy; wait and try again. Some albums may not appear.

Tap **Cornerstone BETA** for the current limitations. Tap **Send feedback** to create a copyable note, then send it to Tre through a channel you already use. The website does not transmit that note. Please do not include credentials, private listening history, or personal information.

Artwork permissions for animation and downloadable wallpapers have not been cleared. Small-scale or noncommercial testing does not grant rights from Spotify, artists, labels, or other artwork owners. Please keep test wallpapers personal; do not redistribute them. This is a beta choice, not a claim of provider approval.

When testing on a phone, also try scrolling the album picker, tapping two tiles to swap, reopening a saved project, and downloading a wallpaper. Tell Tre which browser/device you used if a step fails.
